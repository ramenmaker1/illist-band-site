Discover Our Story

Illist’s story begins in 2017 when drummer Cecil Johnson (formerly of BoughtXBlood) envisioned a studio project that would grow into a full‑fledged band. After months honing their sound, the group debuted with the single Padlock in 2018【770194645182106†L30-L35】. The early line‑up – vocalist Micah Elias, guitarist Jerod Folk, bassist Taylor Youstra and drummer Cecil Johnson – spent their formative years perfecting a blend of metalcore and nu‑metal influences, releasing follow‑up single Life Leech the same year.

In 2019 Illist returned with What I’ve Never Felt. Produced by Zach Jones, this single balances heavy riffs with melody. Vocalist Micah Elias explained in a press announcement that the song reflects the moment when someone who has worn a false identity decides to accept who they truly are【967838569701561†L528-L530】.

After taking time to write and refine their sound, the band resurfaced in 2024–2025 with singles such as Hollow, Malfunction, God’s Land, Sonder and Glass House. These songs embrace an active‑rock and nu‑metal groove while staying true to Illist’s metalcore roots. Today Illist operates out of Culpeper, Virginia with members Micah Elias (vocals), Carson Sekol (guitar), Tyler James (bass) and Yanni Allen (drums). Former members Cecil Johnson, Jerod Folk and Taylor Youstra are acknowledged for their foundational contributions.
