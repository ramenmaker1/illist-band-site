ABOUT ILLIST

Illist is a metalcore/nu-metal band rooted in Virginia, forging a unique sound that blends crushing breakdowns with modern metal intensity.

An early article from The Metal Resource noted that the band spent months shaping their sound before introducing their explosive first lineup to the world. With relentless energy and precision, Illist has established themselves as a force in the metalcore scene.

The band's dedication to their craft and their Virginia roots shine through in every performance, creating an unforgettable experience for fans of heavy music.
