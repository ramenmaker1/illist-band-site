Jerod Folk – Guitar (Past Member)

Jerod Folk was part of Illist’s original line‑up, handling guitar duties alongside Micah’s vocals and Taylor’s bass. As a founding guitarist, Jerod helped define the early sound heard on debut singles Padlock and Life Leech, crafting riffs that combined relentless breakdowns with catchy hooks.

After contributing to the band’s initial releases, Jerod departed to pursue other musical projects. His influence can still be felt in Illist’s aggressive rhythms and melodic sensibilities.
