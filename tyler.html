Tyler James – Bass

Tyler James provides the low‑end foundation that anchors Illist’s rhythm section. Joining during the band’s resurgence, Tyler combines thick, distorted bass tones with melodic runs to complement Carson’s guitar riffs. His playing shines in tracks like Hollow and God’s Land, where the bass lines add depth and groove.

Known for his laid‑back personality and sharp sense of humour, Tyler often shares behind‑the‑scenes glimpses of the band on social media.

Follow Tyler on Instagram: @mr_tylerjames.
