Yanni Allen – Drums

Yanni Allen joined Illist as the band’s new drummer and has quickly become known for his dynamic, groove‑oriented style. Hailing from Norfolk, Virginia, Yanni incorporates hip‑hop rhythms and progressive fills into the band’s breakdowns, giving songs like Sonder and Malfunction their infectious bounce.

Outside of Illist, Yanni collaborates with vocalist Micah on drum playthrough videos and demonstrates a passion for mixing genres. His creativity behind the kit keeps the band’s rhythm section fresh and unpredictable.

Follow Yanni on Instagram: @yanniallen.
