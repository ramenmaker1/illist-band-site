Carson Sekol – Guitar

Carson Sekol joined Illist during the band’s 2024 revival. His guitar work balances nu‑metal groove and rhythmic chugs with metalcore aggression, helping to evolve the band’s sound on recent releases like Malfunction and Glass House. Carson’s playing introduces syncopated riffs and textured atmospheres that complement Micah’s vocals.

While not part of the original line‑up, Carson quickly became integral to Illist’s writing process. His influences range from early 2000s nu‑metal to modern metalcore, and he brings a fresh energy to the live show.

Follow Carson on Instagram: @carsonsekol.
