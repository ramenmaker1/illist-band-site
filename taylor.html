Taylor Youstra – Bass (Past Member)

Taylor Youstra served as Illist’s original bassist during the band’s formative years. Alongside Jerod, Taylor helped lay the rhythmic foundation for early tracks like Padlock and Life Leech. His driving bass lines and stage presence contributed to the raw intensity of the band’s initial shows.

Taylor departed after the early releases but remains an important part of Illist’s history. His work laid the groundwork for the groove‑focused bass approach heard in later singles.
